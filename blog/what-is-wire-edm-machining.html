Technical Insights / What Is Wire EDM Machining?

What Is Wire EDM Machining?

Wire EDM (electrical discharge machining) uses a thin conductive wire to cut complex profiles in hardened tooling steels and other conductive materials. The process is non-contact, which helps preserve delicate walls, narrow slots, and sharp internal corners that are difficult to achieve with conventional milling alone.

For mold programs, wire EDM is commonly applied to inserts, punches, cavities, and precision mold components where profile accuracy and surface integrity matter. Because cutting happens without mechanical force, thin sections and fine details are less likely to deflect during machining.

RIXIN applies wire EDM alongside CNC machining, EDM, and grinding to support automotive, medical, and industrial export projects. Early process planning helps match the right technology to each feature so tolerances, lead time, and cost stay aligned with your drawing requirements.
