About Us

Our Story

Since 2004, RIXIN has grown from a precision mold shop in Dalian into a contract manufacturing partner serving automotive, medical, and industrial OEMs worldwide.

Our teams combine injection molding, die casting, stamping dies, and precision mold components with wire EDM, CNC machining, and integrated tooling workflows.

From prototype through volume production, we focus on DFM, stable quality systems, and responsive export programs that help global customers launch products on schedule.
